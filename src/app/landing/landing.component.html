About TeamWork International

At TeamWork International, we're more than just a recruitment agency — we're your career partner, dedicated to helping jobseekers like you achieve life-changing opportunities in the Gulf region.

Licensed in Egypt (License No. 1313), we specialize in connecting skilled professionals from Egypt with reputable employers in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, and Oman.

With years of experience in international recruitment, our mission is to open doors for ambitious individuals seeking stable, rewarding jobs abroad. We understand the challenges of working overseas — that's why we provide full guidance and support every step of the way, from your initial application to your safe arrival and beyond.
